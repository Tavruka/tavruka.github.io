Privacy policy — Tavruka

Updated 20 October 2026

Controller and contact

Sergio Matos, Portugal, is the controller for personal data processing associated with Tavruka. Support and privacy requests: tavrukaapp@gmail.com.

Progress and preferences

The game stores completed levels and language, sound, music, vibration, tutorial and graphics preferences on your device. It does not require a Tavruka account. We use this information to provide game features and retain progress. Android may include local data in its backup and device transfer features, depending on your device and Google account settings. Tavruka does not provide its own cloud save service.

Purchases and technical services

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play through Unity In-App Purchasing (IAP). The game receives and stores the purchased product, receipt and transaction identifiers to verify and restore access. We do not request payment card numbers or have access to your Google account password. Google and Unity may process information needed for their store, security and diagnostic services, including purchase information, installation or session identifiers, technical device information and approximate country information. Processing depends on the active features and integrations. The game tells Unity services that optional advertising and analytics purposes are not permitted; this does not eliminate technical processing needed for purchases. Tavruka contains no integrated advertising or its own behavioural analytics feature. Provider information: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy .

Email contact

When you email us, we process your email address, message and information needed to resolve your request. Our support email uses Google's Gmail. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or identification documents. We may request only information necessary to verify a purchase or a rights request.

Purposes and legal bases

We use necessary data to provide the game and purchases and answer service-related requests (performance of a contract); to prevent fraud and protect services, where applicable, on the basis of legitimate interests balanced against your rights; and to comply with legal obligations. Any feature requiring consent must obtain it before the relevant processing. Access to free gameplay does not depend on agreeing to marketing.

Recipients, transfers and security

The controller uses Google for email and Google Play, and Unity for purchase integration. These providers may operate outside the European Economic Area under their policies and applicable legal safeguards, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where relevant. You can request information about safeguards using the contact above. We do not sell personal data or provide it for advertising. Local data is stored in the application space subject to Android protections. Purchase service connections use the providers' security mechanisms. The application configures a block on unencrypted HTTP traffic. No system guarantees absolute security; local receipts are neither a payment vault nor server-side purchase validation.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until you clear application data or uninstall, subject to system-managed backups. Local purchase records maintain access; Google's records follow Google's rules and are not deleted when you remove the game. Support messages are retained while the request is handled and for up to 12 months after closure, unless specific information must be retained for justified legal obligations or legal claims. You can clear local data in Android Settings; this erases local progress. It does not cancel purchases or erase third-party records. Eligible purchases can be restored using the same Google account. Email us to request deletion of support data under our control. Requests concerning a Google account or providers' own data should also be sent to those providers.

Your rights and minors

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing. We respond within statutory deadlines, normally one month; proportionate identity verification may be necessary. You may complain to Portugal's CNPD (https://www.cnpd.pt/) or your local competent authority. The intended audience is aged 13 and above; the store rating is determined through its content questionnaire. The game is not intended for children under 13. If a child sends support data without required authorisation, a legal guardian can contact us so we can handle it appropriately. Purchases by minors are subject to applicable authorisations and Google Play controls.

Changes

We will update this policy when features or practices change and indicate the new date. Material changes will be communicated in the game or store listing where appropriate. The text is available in Tavruka Settings even without an Internet connection.
