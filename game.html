THE GAME

Your strategy.
Your last stand.

Action and tower defense come together around one objective: keep the fire alive.

ON THE BATTLEFIELD

A plan is only as good
as your last stand.

Tavruka brings you down to the battlefield. Shape the horde’s path with defenses, then move your heroine to where the fight needs you most.

01 /

Build your position.

Place towers beside the road. Use palisades to hold the line and make every approach count.

PLAN THE BATTLE
02 /

Make them slow down.

Combine mud, fire and tower defenses. Spend your level’s coins where they can turn the tide.

SHAPE THE FIGHT
03 /

Lead from the ground.

Move your heroine as the pressure shifts. A new route or a stronger enemy can change your next move.

TAKE CONTROL

THROUGH THE AGES

One fire.
Four ages.

The full game spans the Tribal, Bronze, Iron and Medieval Ages. The browser demo includes the first 15 levels, through the Iron Age. The bonfire remains at the heart of your defense.

I

Tribal Age

Where the journey begins, with the bonfire at the centre of your stand.

II

Bronze Age

The journey continues into a new age of the battlefield.

III

Iron Age

Hold your ground as Tavruka’s world moves into the Iron Age.

IV

Medieval Age

The full game continues through five medieval battlefields. This age is outside the browser demo.
